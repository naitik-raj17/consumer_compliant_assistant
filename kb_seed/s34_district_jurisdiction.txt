Section 34 (Jurisdiction of District Commission): The District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission hears complaints where the value of goods or services paid as consideration does not exceed the notified limit, presently fifty lakh rupees. A complaint may be filed where the opposite party resides or carries on business or has a branch office, where the cause of action arose wholly or partly, or where the complainant resides or personally works for gain. This allows consumers to file close to home.
