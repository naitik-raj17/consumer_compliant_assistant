Section 47 (Jurisdiction of State Commission): The State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission hears complaints where the value of goods or services paid as consideration exceeds fifty lakh rupees but does not exceed two crore rupees, as presently notified. It also hears complaints against unfair contracts up to that limit, appeals against orders of District Commissions in the State, and can call for records and revise orders of a District Commission that acted without or beyond jurisdiction or with material irregularity.
