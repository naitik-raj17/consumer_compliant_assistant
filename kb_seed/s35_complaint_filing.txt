Section 35 (Manner of filing a complaint): A complaint about goods sold or services provided may be filed before the District Commission by the consumer, by a recognised consumer association, by one or more consumers with the same interest on behalf of all, or by the Central or State Government or the Central Authority. The complaint may be filed electronically, for example through the e-Daakhil portal, and must be accompanied by the prescribed fee. It should state the facts, the defect or deficiency, the supporting documents and the relief sought.
