Section 2(47) (Unfair trade practice): An unfair trade practice is any unfair method or deceptive practice adopted to promote the sale, use or supply of goods or services. Examples include falsely representing the standard, quality, grade or newness of goods; misleading claims about price, warranty or guarantees; false offers of gifts or prizes; selling goods that do not meet safety standards; hoarding to raise prices; refusing to take back defective goods or withdraw deficient services and refund the price within the stated or a reasonable period; not issuing a proper bill or receipt; and disclosing a consumer's personal information without consent.
