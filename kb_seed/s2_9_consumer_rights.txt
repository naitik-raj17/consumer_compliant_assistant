Section 2(9) (Consumer rights): Consumers have the right to be protected against marketing of goods, products or services that are hazardous to life and property; the right to be informed about quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard and price so as to guard against unfair trade practices; the right to be assured, wherever possible, of access to a variety of goods and services at competitive prices; the right to be heard and to have their interests considered at appropriate fora; the right to seek redressal against unfair or restrictive trade practices and unscrupulous exploitation; and the right to consumer awareness.
