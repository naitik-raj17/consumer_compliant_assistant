Section 58 (Jurisdiction of National Commission): The National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, sitting in New Delhi, hears complaints where the value of goods or services paid as consideration exceeds two crore rupees, as presently notified, and complaints against unfair contracts above that value. It also hears appeals against orders of State Commissions and the Central Authority, and may revise orders of State Commissions in cases of jurisdictional error or material irregularity.
