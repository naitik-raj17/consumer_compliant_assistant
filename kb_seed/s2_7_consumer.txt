Section 2(7) (Who is a consumer): A consumer is a person who buys goods or hires or avails services for a price that is paid, promised, partly paid or payable under a deferred payment plan. It includes anyone who uses the goods or benefits from the services with the buyer's approval. Purchases made online, through teleshopping, direct selling or multi-level marketing are covered. A person who buys goods or services for resale or for a commercial purpose is excluded, but buying for earning a livelihood through self-employment is not treated as commercial purpose.
